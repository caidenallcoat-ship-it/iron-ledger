    How to do everything

    Set-up, execution, and the one fault that catches most people. Read it sat down, not at half six with a bell in your hand.

    The programme

    Five sessions in a fixed cycle. You train on whatever days suit; the next session is always the next one in the loop, so nothing gets skipped just because you never train on a Thursday. The target above is how many you owe each week. Eight-week block; the volume climbs.
